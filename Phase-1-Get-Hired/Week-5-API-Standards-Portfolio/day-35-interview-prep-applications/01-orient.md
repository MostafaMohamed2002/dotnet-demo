# ORIENT

## Why this exists
The final transition from "learning" to "earning" happens during the technical interview. Being a great coder is only half the battle; being able to *articulate* your architectural decisions is what gets you the offer. This session is designed to transform your implementation knowledge into "Interview Speak"—the ability to explain complex concepts concisely and confidently to a senior engineer.

## Android/Kotlin Parallels
In the Android world, you've likely faced questions about the Activity Lifecycle or how `ViewModel` survives configuration changes. In the .NET world, the questions shift toward the **Request Pipeline**, **Memory Management (GC)**, and **Data Persistence**. The goal is the same: prove you understand *why* a tool is used, not just *how* to use it.

## The Big Picture
This is the "Closing Loop." We move from the IDE to the CV. We will drill the most common .NET mid-level interview questions using the Clinic API as your primary evidence. If you can point to a specific line of code in your project to justify a conceptual answer, you move from "candidate" to "expert."
